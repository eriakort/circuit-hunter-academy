# Chapter 10 — Presentation interactive labs

This increment adds two bilingual interactive activities to the existing Grade 9 Computer Science Chapter 10 missions.

## Mission mapping

- `c10m1` — Stage Craft: Designing Slides (sections 10.1–10.3): a live slide designer with title/message editing, visual support choices, theme contrast and heading-size controls. Learners practise one idea per slide, readable text, consistent design and copyright attribution.
- `c10m2` — Unit 2 Finale: The Live Presentation (sections 10.4–10.5 + task): transition versus object-animation controls, optional audio/video, an explicitly fictional open-data example, remote-presentation readiness checks and a four-step rehearsal sequence.

## Learning and safety notes

- These are educational simulations and do not create, export or transmit presentation files.
- The data table contains invented example values and is clearly labelled; it is not a real survey or an external open dataset.
- Learners are reminded to verify image/media licences, cite real datasets and check accessibility.
- Remote presentation checks are local learning prompts only; they do not access the microphone, camera, screen or meeting software.
- All new UI labels, guidance and status messages are bilingual.

## Verification

Playwright covers both missions in English and Italian, live slide preview updates, theme and visual selection, transition/animation distinctions, remote readiness feedback and rehearsal progress.
