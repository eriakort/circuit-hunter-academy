The crosswalk stores the counts by grade and strand so the inventory cannot silently drift.\n\n## Current repository evidence\n\nThe initial baseline contained **80 unique exact indicator IDs** in existing JHS2/JHS3 mission metadata. This increment adds the the first six Basic 7 indicator records in Diversity of Matter (Strand 1), plus all ten Cycles (Strand 2) indicators, with source page, candidate lesson-scene references, and assessment references where found. All 16 Basic 7 rows are still marked `needs-review`.\n\n| Grade | Official indicators | Explicit IDs in mission metadata | Not explicitly referenced |\n|---|---:|---:|---:|\n| Basic 7 | 52 | 16 | 36 |\n| Basic 8 | 45 | 33 | 12 |\n| Basic 9 | 53 | 47 | 6 |\n\nAn indicator code appearing in a mission's metadata is only a candidate link. It does not prove that the lesson scenes teach every required action or that a quiz question assesses it. For that reason, all 80 candidate links are initially marked `needs-review`; none is labelled `covered`.\n\n## What this increment adds\n\n- A versioned TypeScript source of truth for official indicator totals across all 15 grade-strands.\n- Candidate indicator-to-mission links extracted from existing JHS2/JHS3 mission metadata.\n- A coverage roll-up that calculates explicit references and unreferenced totals per grade.\n- A regression test that prevents the audit from overstating verified coverage.\n\n## Gaps surfaced\n\n1. **Basic 7:** Strands 1 and 2 now have all 16 official indicator records. The periodic-table indicator has no matching mission; Strand 2 exposes clear gaps for the housefly, domestic-animal classification and animal-use outcomes. Strands 3–5 still need their official indicator records.\n2. **Basic 8:** 12 of 45 official indicators have no entry in the crosswalk.\n3. **Basic 9:** 6 of 53 official indicators have no explicit indicator-level reference in mission metadata.\n4. **All grades:** scene-level and question-level evidence has not yet been manually verified. Candidate links remain `needs-review`.\n\n## Next work\n\nContinue transcribing the remaining Basic 7 Strand 3–5 indicator records strand by strand from the NaCCA PDF, then verify each row against specific lesson-scene indexes and quiz-question indexes. Only after that review should statuses change to `covered`, `partial`, or `not-yet-covered`.
