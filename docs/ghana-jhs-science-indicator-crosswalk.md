# Ghana JHS Science indicator crosswalk — baseline\n\n**Source curriculum:** NaCCA, *Science Common Core Programme Curriculum for JHS1 (B7)–JHS3 (B9)*, September 2020.\n\nOfficial PDF: https://nacca.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/SCIENCE.pdf\n\n## Official indicator totals\n\n| Basic grade | Strand 1 | Strand 2 | Strand 3 | Strand 4 | Strand 5 | Total |\n|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|\n| Basic 7 / JHS1 | 6 | 10 | 8 | 21 | 7 | 52 |\n| Basic 8 / JHS2 | 6 | 8 | 7 | 13 | 11 | 45 |\n| Basic 9 / JHS3 | 7 | 11 | 7 | 16 | 12 | 53 |\n| **Total** | **19** | **29** | **22** | **50** | **30** | **150** |\n\nThese totals are transcribed from the NaCCA curriculum index and must be reconciled against the official PDF during the next verification pass. The crosswalk stores the counts by grade and strand so the inventory cannot silently drift.\n\n## Current repository evidence\n\nThe existing mission metadata contains **80 unique exact indicator IDs** linked to candidate missions:\n\n| Grade | Official indicators | Explicit IDs in mission metadata | Not explicitly referenced |\n|---|---:|---:|---:|\n| Basic 7 | 52 | 0 | 52 |\n| Basic 8 | 45 | 33 | 12 |\n| Basic 9 | 53 | 47 | 6 |\n\nAn indicator code appearing in a mission's metadata is only a candidate link. It does not prove that the lesson scenes teach every required action or that a quiz question assesses it. For that reason, all 80 candidate links are initially marked `needs-review`; none is labelled `covered`.\n\n## What this increment adds\n\n- A versioned TypeScript source of truth for official indicator totals across all 15 grade-strands.\n- Candidate indicator-to-mission links extracted from existing JHS2/JHS3 mission metadata.\n- A coverage roll-up that calculates explicit references and unreferenced totals per grade.\n- A regression test that prevents the audit from overstating verified coverage.\n\n## Gaps surfaced\n\n1. **Basic 7:** no exact indicator IDs are currently recorded in mission metadata. Its mission content is present, but the metadata must be enriched against the official indicator list.\n2. **Basic 8:** 12 of 45 official indicators have no explicit indicator-level reference in mission metadata.\n3. **Basic 9:** 6 of 53 official indicators have no explicit indicator-level reference in mission metadata.\n4. **All grades:** scene-level and question-level evidence has not yet been manually verified. Candidate links remain `needs-review`.\n\n## Next work\n\nComplete the canonical 150-row indicator inventory directly from the official PDF, then review each indicator against specific lesson-scene indexes and quiz-question indexes. Only after that review should statuses change to `covered`, `partial`, or `not-yet-covered`.
