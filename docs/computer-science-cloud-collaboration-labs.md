# Computer Science interactive labs: cloud computing and collaboration

## Scope

This increment adds mission-specific practice to Grade 9 Computer Science Chapter 7. It complements the existing bilingual lessons and mastery checks for missions `c7m1` and `c7m2`.

## Mission-to-lab mapping

| Mission | Learning focus | Interactive practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `c7m1` — Castle in the Clouds | Cloud computing, SaaS/PaaS/IaaS, shared files and folders, access permissions and link exposure | Compare cloud service models; select viewer, commenter or editor permissions; contrast invited-only access with anyone-with-the-link sharing |
| `c7m2` — Team Strike: Calendars & Video Calls | Shared calendars, invitations, video-call preparation, collaboration etiquette and environmental impact | Sequence meeting preparation; check camera and microphone readiness; explore how a remote meeting may avoid travel while still using energy |

## Learning and safety notes

- Learner-facing controls and feedback are bilingual (English/Italian).
- The sharing activity is a teaching model; it does not connect to a cloud account or share real files.
- The environmental slider is an illustrative travel-distance model, not a carbon-footprint calculator.
- The activities reinforce least-privilege access, checking link audiences, and testing equipment before an important call.

## Verification

Playwright coverage checks cloud service model explanations, permission and link-audience feedback, the meeting workflow, pre-call checks, and Italian translations. Repository CI remains the merge gate for the production build, helper tests, question-bank tests and the complete E2E suite.
